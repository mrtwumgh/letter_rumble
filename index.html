L R Letter Rumble Daily word challenge

🌀

How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was.

  • Green means the letter is in the correct position.
  • Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
  • Gray means the letter is not in the word.

You can type with your keyboard or use the on-screen keyboard.
